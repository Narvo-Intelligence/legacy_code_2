# Narvo Voice & Messaging — Narvo Copy System v1.0

## Core Brand Voice
- **The Voice**: A "sharp global sabi-person" — informed, warm, calm, conversational, and unmistakably Nigerian.
- **Personality**: Intelligent, Clear, Emotionally Aware, Editorial, Mobile-native, Locally grounded.
- **Narrative Role**: The trusted friend who already sorted the noise; the calm radio host who knows what matters.

## Tone Modes
- **Warm / Narrative**: (Onboarding, Briefing, Playback, Loading, Toasts, Notifications)
  - Characteristics: Human, companion-like, slightly expressive.
  - Examples: "Wake up, Narvo has the gist.", "Keep this for later."
- **Clear / Assured**: (Navigation, Labels, Dashboard cards, Voice selection, Settings)
  - Characteristics: Short, stable, low-drama.
  - Examples: "Trending Now", "Your Narvo Voice", "Available Offline"
- **Precise / Responsible**: (Truth Tag, Fact-checking, Source attribution, Corrections)
  - Characteristics: Exact, transparent, non-performative.
  - Examples: "What Narvo could verify", "Confidence: 75% based on current sources"

## Copy Principles
- **Explain First, Stylize Second**: Prioritize understanding over mystery. (e.g., "Hear the full gist" > "Truth loaded").
- **Never Overclaim**: Avoid "100% confirmed" or "The real truth". Prefer "Confidence: 85% based on 5 sources".
- **Warm, But Controlled**: Use Nigerian flavour (e.g., "Oya", "gist") naturally, avoid forced slang in trust surfaces.
- **Story Arc Rule (Hook → Meat → Takeaway)**: Lead with why the user should care, then the facts, then what it means for them.

## Approved Vocabulary
- **Core**: gist, briefing, story, listen, source, verified, cross-checked, pulse, clarity, voice, what matters.
- **Warm**: Oya, hold tight, here's the gist, come back to it.
- **Restrained**: Oga, vibe, receipts, sabi, sharp-sharp (use only in onboarding/marketing, never in Truth Tag).

## Banned Phrasing
- **Mechanical**: "initializing pipeline", "synthesizing narrative asset", "processing request".
- **Generic AI**: "AI-powered insights", "cutting-edge intelligence", "next-generation news".
- **Overclaiming**: "Absoutely confirmed", "100% factual".
- **Corporate**: "optimize your workflow", "unlock the power of".
