# Narvo — Product Context

## Mission & Vision
Narvo is a bold, audio-first news platform for Africa. It aims to bridge the gap between fragmented news sources and busy listeners by providing a "Spotify for News" experience. 

## Key Differentiators
- **Authentic Voices**: High-quality TTS with regional Nigerian accents (Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo, Nigerian Pidgin) via YarnGPT.
- **Story Synthesis**: AI-driven clustering and synthesis of multiple articles into a single cohesive narrative.
- **Privacy & Speed**: A focus on sub-5s response times, offline audio playback via PWA, and security via Supabase RLS.

## Core Product Surfaces
- **Dashboard**: High-level feed for scanning stories.
- **News Detail**: In-depth story analysis and multi-language audio.
- **Morning Briefing**: A curated, broadcast-style daily digest (intro → stories → outro).
- **Library**: Offline access to bookmarked and cached stories.

## Target Languages
Narvo supports 5 primary broadcast languages for its launch:
1. **English** (`en`)
2. **Yoruba** (`yo`)
3. **Hausa** (`ha`)
4. **Igbo** (`ig`)
5. **Nigerian Pidgin** (`pcm`)

## Brand Atmosphere (v5)
The visual identity is defined by the **Narvo Design Foundation v5**:
- **Petrol Teal**: Trust, synthesis, structural identity.
- **Signal Vermilion**: Urgency, playback, "Live" moments.
- **Editorial Typography**: Pairing `Newsreader` (serif) with `Instrument Sans`.
